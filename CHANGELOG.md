## 0.1.0 (2026-09-30)

The baseline: everything built before releases were versioned, up to commit `da08bb8`.

### Features

* Multi-tenant foundation: JWT sign-in, tenant isolation enforced by PostgreSQL row-level
  security under a restricted database role, and multi-company scoping of every read and write
* Platform console for creating and administering tenants
* Organization and people: legal entities, locations, org units, jobs, persons, employments,
  dated assignments and personnel actions, configurable employee IDs
* Time and attendance: punches, daily attendance, policy templates, alternate Saturdays,
  overtime review, attendance corrections with approval
* Leave: types, a bucket ledger with monthly accrual, policy sets resolved by employee,
  department, company and tenant, applications with multi-level approval, comp-off,
  encashment and year-end close
* Employee KYC with access logging, onboarding cases, reports and dashboards
* Account security: sign-in throttling and lockout, forced password change, token revocation
* ERP import and tenant migration scripts

### Performance

* Leave accrual credits a whole month set-based (217 s → 9 s for eight runs at 2,000 employees)
* Leave application list paged by keyset, with approval history fetched in one query
* Tenant-wide attendance indexes and an inlinable row-level-security helper
